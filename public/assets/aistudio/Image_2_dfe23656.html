[ Our View ]

Brand value is made in culture
before it shows up in sales

A brand's position is the sum of thousands of small judgments: what people notice, what they repeat, what they would be seen with, and what they pay for.

Survey panels capture part of that. Sales data captures another part. Neither explains why a brand gains or loses ground, because the cause usually sits in culture.

Cipher reads all of it together and scores a brand across five dimensions, with each score traced back to the signals that produced it.

[ Deliverables ]

What every assessment includes

Four outputs land with every brand in the index, every quarter.

Five dimension scores

Brand Codes, Cultural Currency, Social Status, Commercial Appeal, and Customer Lifecycle, each on a 0 to 100 scale.

Signal evidence

Every score links to the press, social, and search signals and the sales outcomes behind it.

Competitive view

The brand placed against up to four competitors and its category average.

Action plan

What to protect, what to strengthen, and what to reconsider before the next quarter.
